Company Settings

When set, every payslip PDF (and a ZIP of the month) is automatically saved here — in a per-month subfolder — each time payroll is processed, in addition to the normal browser download. This is a folder on the machine running this app, not your browser's download folder (browsers don't allow websites to control that).

UANs the EPFO portal itself has recorded as excluded from the Pension Scheme (EPS) — typically members who first joined EPF on or after 1 Sept 2014 on wages above the ceiling at a prior employer. The ECR file reports Rs 0 EPS wages/contribution for these UANs, with their full EPF contribution going to the EPF-share difference instead. You'll only find out a UAN needs to be added here when the EPFO portal rejects an ECR upload citing it.
